Jild 2: Fail (Verbs) aur Tenses Ka Aasaan Nizam

Jild 1 mein humne Naam (Ism) aur Ishara seekha. Ab Jild 2 mein hum Quran ke sabse ahem hissa "Fail" (Actions/Verbs) seekhenge.

1. Fail (Verb) Kya Hai?

Fail woh lafz hai jo kisi kaam (action) ko zahir kare. Jaise:

  • عَلِمَ — Alima — Usne jaana (He knew)
  • كَتَبَ — Kataba — Usne likha (He wrote)
  • قَالَ — Qala — Usne kaha (He said)

💡 Tip: Arabic mein Fail hamesha 3 huroof (letters) se banta hai. Jaise ك ت ب (K-T-B) ka matlab hai "Likhna".

2. Zamaana (Tenses) — Mazi aur Mustaqbil

Fail ki 2 bunyadi qismen hain jo waqt batati hain:

فَعَلَ

Mazi (Past Tense)

Guzray hue waqt ka kaam
(Jaise: Usne kiya)

يَفْعَلُ

Mustaqbil (Present/Future)

Aane wala waqt ya abhi ka kaam
(Jaise: Woh karta hai)

3. Fail ka Kirdar (Doer) — Mafool (Object)

Jab koi kaam hota hai, toh uske 3 hissay hote hain:

  • Fail: Kaam karne wala (Karta).
  • Mafool: Jis par kaam hua (Object).
  • Fail (Verb): Jo kaam hua (Action).

Misaal: ضَرَبَ زَيْدٌ الْكَلْبَ — Zaid ne kuttay ko maara. (Yahan Zaid Fail hai, Kutta Mafool hai, aur Maarna Fail/Verb hai).
