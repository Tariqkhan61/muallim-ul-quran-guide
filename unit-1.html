Jild 1 Unit 1

Bunyadi Asool: Ism, Zameer aur Ishara

Is unit mein hum Quran ke sabse aasaan aur bunyadi alfaz seekhenge — jinse hum jumlay (sentences) banana shuru karte hain.

1. Ism (Naam / Noun) Kya Hai?

Ism woh lafz hai jo kisi cheez, shakhs, ya jagah ka naam bataye. Jaise:

  • كِتَابٌ — Kitab (Book)
  • بَيْتٌ — Bait (Ghar / House)
  • قَلَمٌ — Qalam (Pen)

💡 Tip: Arabic mein aksar ism ke aakhir mein tanween (ـٌ) hota hai — jaise كِتَابٌ.

2. Ishara (Demonstrative) — "Yeh" aur "Woh"

Quran mein do sabse aam ishara alfaz hain:

هذا

Haza

Yeh (Qareeb / Near)

ذَلِكَ

Zalika

Woh (Door / Far)

Misaal: هذا كِتَابٌ — Yeh ek kitab hai.

Misaal: ذَلِكَ بَيْتٌ — Woh ek ghar hai.

3. "ال" (Al) Ka Kya Kaam Hai?

Jab hum kisi cheez ko khaas (specific) banana chahte hain, toh ism ke shuru mein ال (Al) lagate hain.

كِتَابٌ

Ek kitab (koi si bhi)

الْكِتَابُ

Woh khaas kitab (Al-Kitab)

💡 Yaad rakhein: ال lagane se "ek" ka matlab khatam ho jata hai aur cheez khaas ho jati hai.

4. Harf-e-Jaar (Prepositions) — Chhote Alfaz, Bara Maqsad

Yeh chhote alfaz jumlay mein rishta batate hain (kahan, kis se, kis par):

  • فِي — Fi — Mein (In)
  • عَلَى — Ala — Par (On)
  • مِنْ — Min — Se (From)

Misaal: فِي الْبَيْتِ — Ghar mein.

Misaal: عَلَى الْكِتَابِ — Kitab par.
