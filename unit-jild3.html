Jild 3: Murakkab Jumlay (Compound Sentences)

Ab tak humne akelay alfaz (Ism) aur kaam (Fail) seekhe. Jild 3 mein hum inko jod kar Quranic jumlay banana seekhenge.

1. Izafi Murakkab (Possessive Compound)

Jab hum do cheezon ko jodte hain ke ek doosri ki ho jaye, toh usko Izafi Murakkab kehte hain. Isme "ka, ki, ke" ka matlab chupa hota hai.

كِتَابُ اللهِ

Kitabullah

Allah ki Kitab

بَيْتُ الرَّجُلِ

Baitur Rajul

Aadmi ka Ghar

💡 Tip: Izafi Murakkab mein pehla lafz hamesha "Al" (ال) ke baghair hota hai, aur doosra lafz "Al" ke saath ya jarr (kaserah) ke saath aata hai.

2. Sifti Murakkab (Adjective Compound)

Jab hum kisi cheez ki tareef (sift) karte hain, toh usko Sifti Murakkab kehte hain. (Sift = Adjective, Mausoof = Noun).

  • رَجُلٌ كَرِيمٌ — Rajulun Kareemun — Nek (shareef) aadmi
  • كِتَابٌ جَدِيدٌ — Kitabun Jadeedun — Nayi kitab

Qaida: Sift (tareef) hamesha Mausoof (cheez) ke mutabiq hoti hai (muzakar/muannas mein).
